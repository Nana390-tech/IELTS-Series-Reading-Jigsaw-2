Welcome, team! 🍯

You and your partner will read different texts about honey. Then you will answer one set of questions together. Half of the answers are in your text and half are in your partner's text, so you need each other!

ستقرأ كل واحدة منكما نصاً مختلفاً عن العسل، ثم تجيبان معاً على الأسئلة نفسها. نصف الإجابات في نصّك والنصف الآخر في نص زميلتك!

Each partner opens the app on her own device. One chooses A, the other chooses B. Don't look at your partner's screen! 🙈

Step 1 · Read and highlight

Read your text carefully. Turn on the highlighter and select the most important facts. You will need to explain them to your partner. Tap a dotted word to see its Arabic meaning.

اقرئي النص بعناية واستخدمي قلم التظليل لتحديد أهم المعلومات. اضغطي على الكلمة المنقطة لمعرفة معناها بالعربية.

Step 2 · Tell your partner 🗣️

Take turns. Reader A speaks first, then Reader B. Use your highlights, but don't read the text aloud. Explain in your own words.

تبادلا الأدوار. لا تقرئي النص بصوت عالٍ، بل اشرحي بكلماتك الخاصة.

Useful sentences

"My text is about …"
"The most surprising fact is that …"
"Did you know that …?"
"Can you say that again, please?" / "What does … mean?"

Tick when you have done it

Step 3 · Team questions 🤝

Answer all 14 questions together. Some answers are in your text and some are in your partner's text. Ask your partner when the answer is not in your text. Then check your answers together.

أجيبا عن الأسئلة معاً. إذا لم تجدي الإجابة في نصّك، اسألي زميلتك!

You can still look at your own text.
